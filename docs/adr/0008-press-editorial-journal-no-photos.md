# The site is presented as Press, an editorial journal on its own paper, and Lessons carry no photos

The site's presentation is Press (see `GLOSSARY.md`): one serif reading column, at most 680px wide, modelled on the static course stylesheet in `~/Desktop/learn-matt-flow/assets/course.css` but rebuilt natively in this site's CSS. Its light paper is a new colour chosen for this site, `#edeae3` (wash `#f8f6f0`, ink `#1c1b18`, muted `#59564e`, rule `#d6d0bf`, accent `#1d5d78`) — deliberately not the previous `#ffffff`, not the model's `#fffef9`, and not the rejected cream `#faf8f4`. Type is Source Serif 4 with JetBrains Mono, loaded through `next/font`; colour scheme follows the system with no toggle, and print is always light. We chose a journal over keeping the GE-style photo bands, bold display headings and alternating light/dark sections because Lessons are long-form reading: photos dictated the layout, cost load time and credits upkeep, and taught nothing the prose and Scenes don't. Lessons therefore carry no photos. That reverses the photo duties in `.scratch/main-flow-and-redesign/spec.md` and `.scratch/course-rewrite/spec.md` (a required photo per Lesson, credits on the page and at `/credits`), and the bold-type decision in `.scratch/lesson-engine/spec.md`. Photos leave by expand, migrate, contract — first optional in the Lessons module, then removed from Lesson headers and templates, then contracted away — with `/credits` permanently redirecting to `/` and `public/photos/` deleted only in the contract step, so every earlier rollback still has its images. We rejected keeping photos as optional decoration forever, because an optional photo still forces band markup, credit plumbing and an authoring duty on every new Lesson for no teaching gain. This ADR does not supersede ADR 0007: typed Markdown blocks stand in full, Scenes ship static in their final state exactly as ADR 0007 records, and the scroll stepper stays deferred future work under ADR 0007's CSS-plus-IntersectionObserver choice. Nothing here changes a Slug (ADR 0004), a question id (ADR 0005), the Comments contract, or any stored data. The full plan is `.scratch/press-redesign/spec.md`, slices 00–09.

**Consequences.** Authoring a Lesson no longer includes choosing, describing or crediting a photo, and `docs/authoring-lessons.md` drops that duty at contract. Old `/credits` links land on `/` instead of 404ing. Re-adding photos later means reversing the contract (restoring `public/photos/` and the header trio from git history), not flipping a setting — which is the point: the journal stays quiet by default, and anyone who wants imagery back must argue for it against this record.

**Addendum (2026-10-28, after the Press review).** As built, Press diverges from the sequencing above in one recorded respect: the whole change — tokens, layout, and photos expand, migrate and contract — was built and reviewed as ONE working tree against `main`, not landed slice by slice. Workers sequenced expand → migrate → contract inside that tree (`test:content` observed at 410 baseline, 411 at expand, 407 at contract), but the slices were not separately merged, deployed or soaked in production, and no record should claim they were: the rollback rungs that depended on separate landings (optional trio, `public/photos/` still present) were never available in a deployed state. Publishing this tree therefore MUST do one of two things before merge: either re-cut the tree into slice commits in the spec's order (expand, then migrate, then contract, with `public/photos/` deleted only in the final commit), landing them in that sequence; or obtain an explicit waiver from the user, recorded against this ADR, accepting the one-slice landing and its thinner rollback (git-history restore). Until one of those happens, the contract end-state stands in the tree, but the expand/migrate/contract discipline of this ADR remains the rule for how it may be published — the fact that the code already looks like the contract state does not by itself satisfy it.
